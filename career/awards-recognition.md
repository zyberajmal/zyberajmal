## What Recognition Means to Me

Awards are not the objective of the work, but they provide useful confirmation that professional impact is broader than the technology being implemented.

The principles I try to maintain across my work are:

- Take ownership of the problem, not only the assigned task
- Communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Share knowledge and document decisions
- Support colleagues when work crosses team boundaries
- Improve processes rather than repeatedly accepting the same operational problem
- Treat security, reliability and usability as shared responsibilities
- Continue learning from both successful projects and difficult situations

These principles also influence how I approach enterprise technology today. My goal is to deliver work that remains useful, understandable and supportable after the original project is complete.

The technical and project evidence behind that work is available through my [Project Portfolio](../projects/README.md) and [Enterprise Capabilities](capabilities.md).
