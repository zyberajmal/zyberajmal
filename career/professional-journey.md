# Professional Journey

My career has developed across infrastructure engineering, data-center operations, hospitality technology, cybersecurity implementation and enterprise security.

## Career Timeline

### 2010–2011 — IT Intern / Systems Administration
Built foundational experience in enterprise IT operations, user support, systems administration and structured technical processes.

### 2011–2014 — IT Infrastructure Support Engineer
Expanded into infrastructure support, systems operations, networking and day-to-day technology delivery.

### 2014–2015 — Data Center Operations Engineer
Worked in a data-center operations environment supporting enterprise systems, availability, operational discipline and controlled change.

### 2015 — IT In-Charge, Hospitality Operations
Took broader ownership of hospitality technology operations, support coordination and service delivery.

### 2017–2021 — Senior IT Executive, Hospitality Operations
Supported multi-property technology operations and strengthened experience across infrastructure, business applications, networking, security and operational continuity.

### 2021–Present — IT Infrastructure & Security Analyst
Focused increasingly on enterprise security implementation, infrastructure hardening, identity, endpoint protection, email security, privileged access, monitoring, governance and documentation.

## How the Journey Connects

My infrastructure background shapes how I approach cybersecurity today. I do not view security as a separate layer added after implementation. I prefer to design it into the wider technology environment so that controls remain practical, supportable and aligned with business operations.

That perspective continues to guide my work across security engineering, implementation, governance, documentation and project delivery.
